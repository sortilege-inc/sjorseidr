Hermetic House sigils — 5th Edition symbols
===========================================

Source: Ars Magica Wiki, "Ars Magica symbols"
        https://arsmagica.fandom.com/wiki/Ars_Magica_symbols
License: CC-BY-SA (Fandom community content)

These are the 4th/5th-edition House symbols. Files are named by House slug:
  bjornaer, bonisagus, criamon, flambeau, guernicus, jerbiton,
  mercere, merinita, tremere, tytalus, verditius, ex-miscellanea

Note: House Diedne has no 5th-edition sigil (the House predates 5e canon as a
destroyed line), so on this site Valdemar's House Diedne displays the
Ex Miscellanea sigil as a stand-in (GM ruling).
